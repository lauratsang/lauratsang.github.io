A little game of snake

A familiar game with a few colorful twists. Eat apples, grow your snake, and see how long you can keep going.

This little browser game has a home among my projects. You can play right here, or open the original game on its own.

Give it a go

You’ll need a desktop keyboard. Click inside the game first so it can receive your key presses.

Open the game full-size ↗ · Your high score is saved in this browser.

How to play

  • Move with the arrow keys or WASD.
  • Eat red apples to increase your score.
  • Yellow apples double your length; purple apples halve it.
  • After a game ends, press Space to start again.
